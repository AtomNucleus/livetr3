# Phase 3 decoding experiments — 2026-10-27

This round tried five changes to how the worker decodes, each behind its own
flag and screened with one scoreboard pass. Only interruptible prefill passed;
it is now the default after an ABBA check. A sixth pass then retried the pause
decode on top of it.

Every pass is one `script/scoreboard.py` run:
- real Gemma 4 E4B 8-bit with MTP
- cap 6 s, silence 600 ms, extend 2 s, cap pause 200 ms
- FLEURS-8, sermon-v1 and sermon-v2, pooled

Only one model worker ran at a time, and no other LiveTR3 worker was loaded.
Outputs are in `dist/logs/phase3-2026-10-27/`.

Latency gates now use the **live-weighted** final latency: each final is
reweighted to the commit-reason mix of the user's last 51 live sessions, which
is 89% silence-ended (see `fleurs_score.py`). Pooled numbers are still
reported. "First target" is the median time to the first caption.

Three block-3 baselines show how much a pass moves on its own:

| Baseline | Live-weighted median | Live-weighted p95 | Gap p95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A1 (pause-decode A1) | 1.57 | 2.50 | 1.86 |
| 3e-before | 1.63 | 2.53 | 1.94 |
| 3e-before-a2 | 1.75 | 2.72 | 2.00 |

Expect about ±0.08 s on the median and gap p95 from noise alone.

## 3e: interruptible prefill — adopted

**How it works.** In the worker child, the language model's class is swapped
for a subclass whose `__call__` raises `GenerationCancelled` when the running
job's cancel flag is set. That call runs once per prefill chunk and once per
decode or MTP verify step, so a cancel takes effect at the next one.

**Who gets it.** Only cancellable jobs, previews today, are given
`prefill_step_size=32`, so their prefill runs as about five chunks instead of
one. Finals keep full-speed prefill, and the hook never fires for them because
nothing arms it.

**Tracing.** `worker_cancel_free` records how long the worker stays busy after
a cancel.

| Pass | Cancel→free median / p95 | Preview prefill median | Final prefill median | Final queue median / p95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before | 0.047 / 0.406 s | 0.393 s | 0.476 s | 0.030 / 0.482 s |
| chunk 32 | 0.035 / 0.072 s | 0.444 s | 0.464 s | 0.023 / 0.089 s |
| chunk 32, B2 | 0.033 / 0.068 s | 0.428 s | 0.451 s | 0.019 / 0.091 s |
| before, A2 | 0.041 / 0.373 s | 0.431 s | 0.515 s | 0.031 / 0.499 s |

(Prefill here is time to the decode's first response.)

The cost is about 45 ms of extra prefill per preview. The gain is that a
committed utterance's final no longer waits out a stale preview's prefill:
final queue p95 drops from about 0.5 s to 0.09 s.

In both ABBA pairs the chunked pass beat both baselines on the live-weighted
median and p95. Gap p95, first target, WER, numbers and negation are the same
or better. Outputs stay within noise, and MTP acceptance and tokens per round
are unchanged (0.67 / 2.34).

`LIVETR3_CANCELLABLE_PREFILL_CHUNK` now defaults to 32; set it to 0 for the
old behavior.

## 3c: instruction-prefix KV cache — spike only

`prefix-spike.json` comes from one model load, with timings taken after a warm
run on each window. It covers 30 windows of 2–6 s from sermon-v2 04–07 and
FLEURS.

The fixed instruction before the audio is 77 of a median 184 prompt tokens.
Medians:

| Step | Time |
| --- | ---: |
| Embedding (audio encoder) | 0.069 s |
| Full language-model prefill | 0.415 s |
| Prefix-only prefill | 0.226 s |
| Suffix prefill on a cached prefix | 0.289 s |

So a cached prefix would save about **0.13 s per decode**, about a quarter of
time to first token. Next-token argmax matched a full prefill in 30 of 30
windows. Logits differ by up to 1.1, the batch non-invariance that mlx-vlm's
own APC notes describe.

It is not implemented. Both of mlx-vlm 0.7.4's reuse paths,
`PromptCacheState` and APC, refuse a cached prefix when media follows it, and
our audio always does. A working version needs:
- its own prefill: embed the full prompt, then run only the suffix on a copied
  prefix cache
- the MTP drafter's prefill capture (hidden states and shared KV) for the
  cached tokens

It is the largest remaining per-decode saving found, and worth a separate
change.

## 3b: LocalAgreement-2 source prefix — rejected

With `LIVETR3_LA2_PREFIX`, the session takes the words the last two complete
previews agree on. It stops short of either preview's last word and needs at
least two words. Those words are appended to the prompt as the start of the
model's answer.

It forced a prefix on 276 previews, with a median of 4 words. It doesn't help
because the translation half of the answer still has to be generated:
- preview generation stays at a median of 16 tokens
- each preview takes longer, 0.97 vs 0.86 s of worker time
- finals queue behind the slower previews

Forcing on finals too (`all`) also raises WER to 4.99%. Both modes fail the
latency gate. Rejected experiments don't go to main, so the code is parked on
the branch `claude/la2-prefix`, with no PR.

## 3d: newest mlx / mlx-vlm — rejected

The newest releases, mlx 0.32.4 and mlx-vlm 0.7.10 (out 2026-10-21), were
installed in a separate venv, `dist/venvs/mlx-0.32.4`. It is a copy of the
shared env's freeze with only those packages changed; mlx-vlm 0.7.10 also pulls
in `datasets` and `pandas`. The shared env was not touched.

The pass ran from a worktree whose `app/backend/.venv` points at that venv, with
chunk 32 on. Against the two chunk-32 passes:

| | New versions | Current versions (chunk-32 passes) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live-weighted median / p95 | 1.51 / 2.38 s | 1.59 / 2.47 and 1.54 / 2.42 s |
| Gap p95 | 1.86 s | 1.95 and 1.88 s |
| Final decode median | 1.17 s | 1.19 s |
| MTP acceptance / tokens per round | 0.67 / 2.33 | 0.67 / 2.33 |
| WER | 4.96% | 4.81% and 4.72% |
| MTP fallbacks | 0 | 0 |

It showed no measured win. Latency was within pass-to-pass noise, and WER rose
0.15–0.24 points. Upgrading would also change the everyday app's engine,
because `dist/main-build`'s venv is a symlink to the shared one. Rejected; any
future upgrade needs an ABBA first.

## Pause decode retest

This pass reran the Phase 2 pause decode on top of Phase 3, using:
- B1's setup: `LIVETR3_PAUSE_DECODE=1`, with the in-flight preview cancelled
- interruptible prefill, the new default. Pause jobs are cancellable, so their
  prefill is chunked and a discarded pause decode frees the worker within one
  chunk.
- a trigger 100 ms later (`LIVETR3_PAUSE_DECODE_DELAY_MS=100`), about 430 ms of
  silence. The window still ends at the 300 ms pad.

It ran from the branch `claude/pause-decode-retest`, which is #25 merged onto
this branch. The ABBA ran in the order B, A, B, A:

| Pass | Live-weighted median | Live-weighted p95 | Gap p95 | First target | WER | Silence-ended finals |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| B1, on | 1.49 | 2.42 | 1.96 | 2.64 | 4.81% | FLEURS 1.43 s, sermon-v2 1.41 s |
| A1, off | 1.52 | 2.39 | 1.85 | 2.52 | 4.69% | FLEURS 1.48 s, sermon-v2 1.44 s |
| B2, on | 1.66 | 2.83 | 2.08 | 2.78 | 4.87% | FLEURS 1.72 s, sermon-v2 1.50 s |
| A2, off | 1.62 | 2.59 | 1.92 | 2.73 | 4.81% | FLEURS 1.74 s, sermon-v2 1.48 s |

The machine got slower over the four passes: preview prefill went from 0.43 s
to 0.45–0.47 s, whatever the flag. Compared pair by pair:
- live-weighted median: −0.03 s and +0.04 s, no gain
- live-weighted p95: +0.03 s and +0.24 s
- gap p95: worse by 0.11 and 0.16 s

**It fails both adoption conditions.** The flag stays off and #25 stays a
draft.

The later trigger did what it was meant to. On sermon-v2, discards for resumed
speech fell from 64 to 29 and starts from 182 to 138. The silence-ended finals
that still got a pause decode all reused it, with no fallbacks. Some short
pauses now commit before the later trigger fires (sermon-v2 109 of 117; the
rest decode fresh).

There is little left to gain, for two reasons. Chunked prefill already removed
most of the final's queue wait, which is what the pause decode was saving:
flag-off silence-ended finals went from 1.66 s (Phase 2 A1, FLEURS) to 1.48 s.
And the pause decode's own lead is about 170 ms, which it often spends queued
behind the previous utterance's final. FLEURS pause queue p95 was 1.9–2.2 s.

Its gain over the A pass beside it is 0.03–0.05 s on silence-ended finals, and
the extra decodes still cost gap p95.
